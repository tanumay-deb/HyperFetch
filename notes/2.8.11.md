**Streams**
- When a site refuses one piece of a stream, that piece is taken from
  another quality of the same stream instead of the download stopping with
  "URL expired". You may see a moment of lower quality; the rest of the
  video stays at the quality you picked.
- A piece that fails for a reason that may pass, such as a timeout or a
  server error, is tried again with longer waits before the download
  gives up.
- One slow piece no longer holds back the pieces after it, so streams from
  slow servers finish sooner.
- Pause on a stream now stops it at once.
- An encrypted stream with a piece that will not decrypt now says which
  piece failed, instead of "Download ended unexpectedly".

**Safer**
- Pressing Enter on the "Download complete" window closes it. A program is
  never started from that window: Folder shows it instead.
- Every file in a finished folder gets Windows' "from the internet" mark,
  even when one file in it cannot take it.
- A damaged .torrent file, or a bad answer from a tracker, can no longer
  freeze HyperFetch.

**Fixes**
- With "Use more than one internet connection" on, a connection plugged in
  after you saved Settings, such as a phone over USB, is used too.
- The check for a new version uses the proxy set in HyperFetch's Settings,
  and checking a downloaded update no longer freezes the window for a
  moment.
- A "Delete from disk" that HyperFetch could not finish before it closed
  keeps any file it cannot be sure about, and says so.
- Waiting torrents use less of the processor.
- The Settings button shows a gear again.

**Browser extension 1.9.7**
- Uses less memory over a long browsing session.

**Known issue**
- YouTube videos that need your sign-in (private or members-only) may fail
  until a fix arrives from yt-dlp's side. Public videos download as
  before.
