Source, with every release
--------------------------
Every HyperFetch release carries, beside its installer and its portable
zip, HyperFetch-<version>-gpl-source.zip: the source of the aria2 and the
FFmpeg that release ships - aria2 1.37.0 and the six libraries its Windows
build is made of (GMP, Expat, SQLite, zlib, c-ares, libssh2), and FFmpeg at
the commit in FFmpeg-version.txt with the scripts of the build that made it.
https://github.com/tanumay-deb/HyperFetch/releases
