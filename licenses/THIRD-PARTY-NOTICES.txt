Written offer
-------------
For at least three years from the date of each HyperFetch release, anyone
may have the complete corresponding source code of the aria2 and FFmpeg that
release ships, at no charge beyond the cost of providing it: ask by opening
an issue at https://github.com/tanumay-deb/HyperFetch/issues and naming the
HyperFetch version.
